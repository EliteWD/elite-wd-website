# Creative brief — Elite W&D Installers

Source of truth for every generated image and video. All creatives must look like
they were shot on the same day, at the same house, by the same photographer.

## The world (consistency bible)

- **Location:** a modern Southwest Florida home (Naples / Cape Coral style) — white
  smooth stucco, flat roofline, travertine deck, pool and lanai, royal palms, Gulf light.
- **Protagonist:** floor-to-ceiling impact glass — **matte black aluminum frames, slim
  sightlines, slightly gray Low-E glass** with clean, soft reflections. Same finish in every shot.
- **Light:** blue hour / dusk for exteriors (matches the site's black stage), warm 2700K
  interior glow; storm scenes are cool steel-blue outside, warm and calm inside.
- **Look:** architectural photography, 35mm full-frame, natural color, gentle contrast,
  no HDR halos, no oversaturation, no lens flares. Photorealistic.
- **Always exclude:** people's faces, text, logos, brand marks, watermarks, visible
  window stickers, cars, clutter.
- **Method:** generate the master exterior keyframe first; every other still uses it as
  an image reference so the house, frames and palette are inherited, not re-invented.

## Rules for video

- Model: newest full Seedance available on the account (not the mini variant).
- Audio off. Draft at low resolution first, master at 1080p only after approval.
- Always quote credits before generating; confirm with the owner first.
